# Privacy policy for Just QR

Last updated: November 2026

## The short version

Just QR collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no server. Everything you make
stays on your device.

## What the app does with your data

- **Your codes.** The text you type (a link, a Wi-Fi password, a contact card...) becomes a
  QR code on your device. The code holds that text directly. Nothing is sent anywhere, and
  nobody, including us, can see who scans it.
- **History.** Codes you save, share, copy or print are kept on your device, in the app's
  own storage (the browser's IndexedDB). You can delete one, or all of them, in History.
- **Logos.** A picture you add to a code is shrunk and kept with that code, on your device
  only.
- **Draft.** What you were typing is kept on your device so it comes back if the app closes.
  "Start fresh" clears it.
- **Settings.** Your language and theme are kept on your device.
- **Saved files.** On Android, "Save" writes the image into your phone's `Documents/Just QR`
  folder. In a browser, it is a normal download. These files are yours: other apps on your
  phone can see them, like any file in Documents.
- **Share.** "Share" hands the image to the app you pick in Android's share sheet (or your
  browser's). What that app does with it is outside Just QR.

## What the app never does

- **No internet.** The Android app does not have the internet permission, so it could not
  send anything even if it tried. The web version only loads its own files.
- **No cloud backup.** Android backup and phone-to-phone transfer are turned off for the
  app's data. A new phone starts with an empty history; your saved image files in
  Documents move with your phone like any other file.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking library is inside
  the app. Every release is checked for this automatically before it is published.
- **No scanning.** The app never uses the camera.

## Permissions

- **Android 11 and newer:** none.
- **Android 10 and older:** storage access, asked once when you first save, so the image
  can be written into Documents. If you say no, you can still share the code.

## The web version

The web app at qr.open-waqf.org is static files served by GitHub Pages. As with any
website, the host sees the request that loads the page (your IP address and browser type).
After that first load the app runs on your device and sends nothing. It works offline.

## Children

Just QR is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new text appears here with a new date. The app itself will
still collect nothing: that is what it is for.
